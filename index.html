Welcome to Book High School

Located in the eastern suburb of Cranborne, Harare, Book High School is a premier Government day school dedicated to fostering academic excellence, technical mastery, and sporting achievement.

Academic Excellence

Offering comprehensive O-Level and Advanced Level blocks with an optimal, low teacher-to-pupil ratio across approximately 500 students.

Technical Foundations

Equipping students with modern, hands-on environments for Technical Graphics, Metal Work, Wood Work, and Art.

Elite Athletics

Proudly competing against top-tier institutions like Prince Edward, St John's, Churchill, and Allan Wilson in rugby, soccer, cricket, basketball, hockey, and swimming.
