Welcome to Book High School

Located in Cranborne, Harare, Book High School is a Government day school committed to academic excellence, technical education, and competitive athletics. We serve roughly 500 students across O-Level and A-Level classes with a focus on discipline, practical skills, and community.

Academic Excellence

Strong curricula from Form 1 to Form 6 with dedicated teachers and structured revision programs for ZIMSEC O-Level and A-Level examinations.

Technical Foundations

Hands-on workshops in Technical Graphics, Metal Work, Wood Work, and Art to build practical competence and problem-solving skills.

Elite Athletics & Clubs

Competitive teams in rugby, soccer, cricket and more, plus clubs for debate, music, and environmental stewardship that develop leadership and teamwork.
