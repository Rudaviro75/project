Sports & Clubs

At Book High School, sport and co-curricular activities build character, discipline, and teamwork alongside academic excellence.

Explore Our Teams

Our Sports

Under Sports Director Mr Samarai, our teams train throughout the year and compete in inter-school and regional fixtures. We field both junior and senior teams across multiple disciplines.

🏉

Rugby

Our flagship sport. Book High rugby teams compete in the Harare Metropolitan Province Schools League. Training runs Monday, Wednesday and Friday afternoons.

Term 1 & 2
⚽

Soccer

Junior and senior boys' teams take part in the NASH inter-school soccer competition. Open trials are held at the start of every term.

Term 1 & 3
🏏

Cricket

Our cricket squad competes in the schools cricket league during the first term. The school provides coaching and equipment for all players.

Term 1
🏃

Athletics

Track and field events including 100 m, 200 m, 400 m, 800 m, 1500 m, long jump, high jump, shot put and javelin. Athletes compete at district and provincial level.

Term 2
🏐

Volleyball

Boys' and girls' volleyball teams play in the inter-school league. Practices are held every Tuesday and Thursday.

Term 2 & 3
♟️

Chess

Our chess team participates in the national schools chess championship. Open to all learners from Form 1 to Form 6.
