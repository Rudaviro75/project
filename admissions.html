How to Apply

1

Submit Enquiry

Visit the school office or email bookhighschool@gmail.com to register your interest.

2

Complete Application

Submit the application form with the documents listed below.

3

Placement Assessment

Pupils write a short assessment in English and Mathematics.

4

Interview

A meeting with the Head and the admissions team.

5

Offer Letter

Successful applicants receive a formal offer from the school.

6

Enrolment

Pay fees at the bursar's office and confirm your place before term begins.

Entry Requirements

For all applicants

  • Completed application form
  • Birth certificate (certified copy)
  • Latest school report or transfer letter
  • Two passport-sized photographs
  • Copy of parent or guardian ID
  • Proof of residence

Depending on the level

  • Grade 7 results slip (Form 1 entry)
  • O-Level results slip (A-Level entry)
