Latest News

10 October 2026 Academic

O-Level Results: 78% Pass Rate

Book High School is proud to announce a 78% pass rate in the 2025 ZIMSEC O-Level examinations. Fifteen learners achieved five or more A grades. The Headmaster congratulated both staff and learners on the excellent results and encouraged the current Form 4 class to aim even higher.

25 September 2026 Sports

Rugby Team Reaches Provincial Semi-Finals

The senior rugby squad, coached under the direction of Sports Director Mr Samarai, defeated three schools in the Harare Metropolitan Province Schools League to reach the semi-finals. The team will face Churchill High School in the next round. Training continues every Monday, Wednesday and Friday afternoon.

15 September 2026 Clubs

Environmental Club Wins Regional Award

Our Environmental Club has been recognised by the Harare Metropolitan Council for its campus tree-planting initiative. Over 200 indigenous trees have been planted on the school grounds in the past two years. The club meets every Friday and welcomes new members from all forms.

1 September 2026 Academic

New Computer Lab Opens

A new 30-station computer lab was officially opened by the District Schools Inspector. The lab will support ICT lessons, typing practice, and online research for both O-Level and A-Level learners. The school thanks the Parents' Committee for funding the project.

20 August 2026 Clubs

Debate Team Qualifies for Nationals

The Debate and Public Speaking Club secured first place at the Harare Province Schools Debate Competition. The team of four Form 5 learners will represent the province at the national finals in Bulawayo next month.

5 August 2026 Sports

Soccer Team Begins NASH Campaign

The senior boys' soccer team kicked off the NASH inter-school competition with a 3-1 victory over a neighbouring school. Open trials for the junior squad will be held at the start of Term 3. All interested learners should see Mr Samarai.
